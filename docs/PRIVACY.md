# Privacy terms

Sima has no servers and no accounts, and collects no analytics. These terms explain what stays on your computer, what leaves it, and who can use each AI provider.

## Age and your birth date

Sima asks for your birth date once, when you set it up.

- **18 or older:** Sima uses Google Gemini's free tier by default. You can switch to a local model through Ollama, or to OpenAI or Anthropic with your own API key.
- **Under 18:** Sima uses a local model through Ollama. Online providers aren't available.
- **Your birth date isn't saved or sent anywhere.** Sima works out your age once, then stores only whether you're 18 or older. Removing the extension deletes that answer.

Sima can't verify your age, so enter your real birth date. Google's terms require API users to be 18 or older.

## Europe: EEA, Switzerland and the UK

**Gemini's free tier isn't available in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom.** Google's terms allow only its paid services for apps offered to users in those regions. Sima can't detect your location, and it asks you to confirm you aren't in those regions before using Gemini.

If you're in one of these regions, choose Ollama in Settings, or an online provider whose terms allow use where you are.

## What stays on your computer

These stay in your browser and are never sent to the Sima project:

- activities Sima has read, answers, explanations and logs
- your settings and API keys, which are stored unencrypted in your Chrome profile

With Ollama, question text also stays on your computer.

## What leaves your computer

With an online provider, and only when you click **Fill answers** or **Explain only**, Sima sends that provider the question text, tables, image descriptions, field labels, the activity's directions, and any example or word list on the page. It uses your own API key. Sima doesn't send your name, your account details or your birth date, but it can't rule out personal details that appear within a page's questions.

## Gemini's free tier

Google's terms for unpaid use say:

- Google may use what you submit, and the responses, to provide, improve and develop its products and machine learning technologies.
- Human reviewers may read, annotate and process what you submit. Google says it disconnects this data from your account, API key and project first.
- Don't submit sensitive, confidential or personal information.

These are Google's terms and they can change, so read the current [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and [pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing). Free-tier rate limits are shown in [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/rate-limit).

## Deleting your data

In Settings, **Delete saved activities** and **Delete API keys** remove that data. Removing the extension deletes everything Sima stored. To delete what a provider received, contact that provider.
